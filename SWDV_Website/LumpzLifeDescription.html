Lumps Life

Build your city and create its residents the Lumps! Customize their homes, outfits, and personalities, then check in to watch their quirky lives unfold. From friendships and romances to horrors untold and other unexpected surprises, you'll find yourself enamoured by their strange little world.

Tons of Customization

The world of the lumps is pretty lonely so it's your job to create a wonderful city full of wonderful people. Lumps is full of options for customization by default from a bikers mohawk to a clowns nose your sure to find something to create your perfect resident. If the built in options aren't enough simply find the pencil icon and start sketching out the perfect hairdo. From resturaunt exteriors to bedroom interiors the control is in the palm of your hand.

Wacky Shenanigans

Watch as your lumps make friends, fall in love, and commit arson. Help the lumps by making decisions for them. Noisy neighbor? Maybe some earmuffs will do, or maybe it's time to move out. Help them decide and watch as the shenanigans unfold—from the park to their dreams, you witness it all.
