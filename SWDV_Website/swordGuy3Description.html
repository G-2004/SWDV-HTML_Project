Sword Guy 2

The action platformer where you try to escape from the depths of tartarus in search of your mother fighting the demons of the underworld and your evil dad Hades with the help of the other olympians. Tear through the tortured souls, beasts of legend, and your everlasting angst in order to find your mother and the truth.

Challenging Combat

Explore the depths of tartarus with your mighty sword gaining abilities and mapping your everchanging surroundings. Find your way to a boss chamber where difficult foes lie. defeat these champions of the underworld to gain acess to the next layer of the depths of the underworld. You'll find friend and foe alike who can put up quite the challenge preventing your exit. Die and find yourself back in your room where you can upgrade certain abilites and keep some around for future runs.

Meet the gods themselves

On your quest to the surface you'll find yourself in league with the gods themselves. You'll have conversations with them and they'll help you along your journey with new abilities. From homing arrows to wider swings their perks are the best shot you have of escaping the underworld so keep up a good reputation.
